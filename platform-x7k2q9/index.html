ABOUT THIS LAYER
OC density 5-15cm · kg/m³
SoilGrids 2.0 (ISRIC)250 mdepth 5-15cmstatic

Organic carbon density (kg/m³): carbon content combined with bulk density and coarse fragments.

Relevant to convert carbon concentration into mass per volume, the step between SOC content and SOC stock.

SoilGrids 2.0 (ISRIC) property ocd, depth 5-15cm, mean prediction, converted to kg/m³

Resolution: one pixel covers 6.25 ha, the median field 0.1 ha. Values describe the area around a field, not the field itself: use them as context or baseline, not as a field measurement.

Static map: one modelled value per pixel, no time series. Changes on the fields are tracked with the Satellite Hook and field measurements.
